## What Is Praenox Free?

Praenox Free converts your daily health data into a personalized soundscape — a layered audio file tuned specifically to your body state on that day. No two soundscapes are the same. The sound is built from your HRV, resting heart rate, sleep quality, and stress level, and its purpose is to help your nervous system stabilize: reducing spikes in stress and heart rate, supporting recovery, and bringing your biometric readings toward a healthier baseline over time.

The more consistently you use it, the more the system understands your patterns and the more targeted each generation becomes.

---

## Before You Start — Important

**Always use earbuds or headphones. Never speakers.**

Praenox Free uses binaural beats, layered frequencies, and stereo-panned audio that only work correctly when each ear receives a separate audio channel. On speakers the layers collapse into each other and the therapeutic effect is lost entirely. In-ear earbuds give the best result; over-ear headphones also work well.

## Windows Security Warning — Why Does It Appear?

When you download or run Praenox Free for the first time, Windows may show a warning:

- **Browser:** "Keep" / "Delete" prompt when downloading
- **SmartScreen:** "Windows protected your PC" before running

**This is expected. The app is safe.**

The warning appears because Praenox Free is not signed with a paid code-signing certificate. Code signing is a system where developers pay a Certificate Authority to verify their identity — Windows then automatically trusts signed apps and shows no warning. This certificate costs hundreds of dollars per year, which is not feasible for a free, independently developed app.

The app itself has not changed. There is no malware, no virus, no hidden code. Windows simply has no way to automatically verify an unsigned executable and warns you by default.

**To run it:**

1. When the browser prompts "Keep" or "Delete" — click **Keep**
2. If SmartScreen appears — click **More info**, then **Run anyway**

You only need to do this once. After the first run Windows remembers your choice.

## Step-by-Step Workflow

### Step 1 — Enter Your Health Data

Go to **Signal Extraction**. Under **Manual Parameters**, enter the following from your watch app or health tracker for the previous night and day:

| Field | What to Enter | Range |
|---|---|---|
| **Date** | The night the data is from | — |
| **HRV ms** | Heart Rate Variability in milliseconds | 1 – 200 ms |
| **HR bpm** | Resting heart rate | 30 – 120 bpm |
| **Deep %** | Deep sleep percentage | 0 – 40% |
| **REM %** | REM sleep percentage | 0 – 40% |
| **Stress** | Daily stress score | 0 – 100 |
| **Sleep** | Total sleep duration (hours + minutes) | 60 – 720 min |

Enter the real numbers from your device — the accuracy of the soundscape depends entirely on the accuracy of the data you put in.

Once all fields are filled, click **Save**. The day will appear in the **Saved Health Days** table below.

---

### Step 2 — Choose Palette and Duration

With a saved day selected from the table, scroll to **Step 3 — Palette + Generate**.

**Palette** is the acoustic texture of your soundscape — the type of sounds layered into it. The system will recommend a palette based on your data, or you can adjust it manually. Categories include nature sounds, instruments (piano, guitar, strings, Tibetan bowls), Gregorian harmonics, solfeggio tones, bass, and more. Each layer's volume is driven by your biometric fingerprint for that day.

**Duration** sets how long the generated file will be. Use the slider or the preset labels:

| Preset | Intended Use |
|---|---|
| **Arrive** | Short session, easing in |
| **Build** | Medium session, building focus or calm |
| **Deep Work** | Extended session for concentration or deep rest |
| **Integrate** | Long wind-down |
| **Close** | Full-length closure session |

You can also type a custom duration in minutes.

**Style** (optional) lets you set a musical character — Ambient, Nature, Classical, Sacred, Jazz, Blues, and others. If left on Auto, the system selects a style based on your data and the date.

---

### Step 3 — Generate

Click **Generate**. Praenox Free analyzes your biometric fingerprint for the selected day and builds a unique soundscape. The generation takes a few seconds.

The system automatically identifies which area of your health needs the most support and assigns a protocol:

| Protocol | Triggered When |
|---|---|
| **Parasympathetic Restore** | General recovery deficit |
| **Delta Induction** | Deep sleep significantly below your baseline |
| **Cortisol Reset** | Stress load is elevated |
| **REM Restore** | REM sleep significantly below your baseline |
| **Cardiac Calm** | Resting heart rate elevated above your baseline |
| **Maintenance** | Recovery score is healthy — reinforcement mode |

The protocol determines the binaural beat frequency, breath guide pacing, bass grounding, and the mix of all layers. You will see the active layers listed under **Step 5 — Analysis** once a soundscape is selected.

---

### Step 4 — Listen

Once generated, your soundscape appears in the **Generated Soundscapes** list. Select it and press **Play** to listen directly in the app through the built-in **Immersion** player.

You can also open **Immersion** from the home screen at any time to access your generated soundscapes and control playback volume.

**Remember: earbuds only.**

---

### Step 5 — Export

To listen on your phone, another device, or offline, export the soundscape as an MP3 file.

Select a soundscape from the list and click the export button. The file will be saved as:

```
Praenox Free_[name]_[date]_[duration].mp3
```

Transfer this file to your phone or any audio player. The file plays as a standard MP3 — use your earbuds and listen in a quiet environment for best results.

## Tracking Progress — Pattern Drift

Open **Pattern Drift** from the home screen to see how your metrics change over time.

- **Progress Charts** show HRV, Recovery, Sleep, and Stress compared to the previous day
- **Timeline Graph** plots your full saved history — zoom in on specific date ranges
- **Day Comparison** lets you pick any two saved days and compare all metrics side by side, with percentage differences shown for HRV, Recovery, Sleep, and Stress

The goal is to see your HRV rising, your stress score falling, and your deep sleep and REM percentages stabilizing. Spikes in stress or drops in HRV become visible in the timeline, and the soundscape generated for those days will have been calibrated specifically to address them.

## Data Vault — Backup and Import/Export

Open **Data Vault** from the home screen to manage your health history.

- **Export** saves your saved days to a file you can store externally
- **Import** loads days back in — the same validation rules apply as manual input
- The app creates an automatic backup each time you close the data panel

Keep backups if you plan to reinstall or move to a new machine.

## Tips for Best Results

- **Enter data daily.** The system builds a personal baseline from your history. The more days you have saved, the more accurate the protocol selection becomes.
- **Listen at a comfortable, moderate volume.** The layers are designed to work at a relaxed listening level — not loud.
- **Use it before sleep or during rest.** The soundscape is not background music — give it your attention, especially during the first few minutes.
- **Do not switch to speakers mid-session.** If you need to stop, pause and resume with earbuds.
- **Be consistent.** The main purpose of Praenox Free is to reduce data spikes and stabilize your biometric readings over time. A single session helps; a regular habit compounds.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why does the free version change protocols almost every day?**

The free version has no persistent Stability Mode and no Sleep Debt detection. It looks only at the health data you entered for that specific day (HRV, resting heart rate, deep sleep %, REM %, stress) and picks the protocol based on which single metric is furthest below your personal baseline. Because your daily biometrics naturally fluctuate, the "worst metric" often changes day to day. The app is not broken — it is faithfully reflecting your daily variability.

**Does the free version learn anything from my history?**

Yes, but only your personal baselines, not behavioural patterns. The free version continuously updates your baselines (normal HRV, normal resting HR, typical deep sleep %, typical REM %, typical stress) using all saved days. These baselines are used to calculate the deficit for each new day.

What the free version does not do:
- Learn long-term patterns (e.g., "your HRV has been falling for 5 days")
- Predict next-day recovery
- Force a protocol across multiple days (like Fusion Stage 2 in the paid version)
- Detect sleep debt or force Delta Induction for chronic undersleep

**Does the free version smooth spikes or try to stabilise my protocol?**

No. The free version has no spike-smoothing rule. If your HRV jumps overnight, the protocol for that day will be based on the new value directly. There is no stable mode that locks you into a single protocol for several days. If you find the protocol changing too often, it is because your biometrics themselves are oscillating — the free version simply shows you that truth.

**I feel like I'm stuck in a random loop. Is the app broken?**

No, it is working as designed. The free version does not analyse chronic conditions — it reacts to each day's data independently. If your daily data shows high stress one day, low REM the next, and good recovery the third, the app will output Cortisol Reset → REM Restore → Maintenance in sequence. That is not a loop — it is a reflection of your changing state. If you want the engine to stop reacting to daily noise and instead respond to your underlying patterns, that is exactly what the paid version (with Fusion, Stability Mode, and Sleep Debt) was built for.

**What protocols does the free version use?**

Six protocols, automatically selected based on your biometric fingerprint:

| Protocol | Trigger |
|---|---|
| **Parasympathetic Restore** | General deficit, no single metric dominates |
| **Delta Induction** | Deep sleep % significantly below your baseline |
| **Cortisol Reset** | Stress score significantly above your baseline |
| **REM Restore** | REM % significantly below your baseline |
| **Cardiac Calm** | Resting heart rate significantly above your baseline |
| **Maintenance** | All metrics near or above baseline — reinforcement mode |

The protocol label appears in the Analysis panel after generation.

**Can I make the free version feel less random?**

Yes — two things help: enter data every day (gaps make the baseline less stable), and look at your raw numbers. If your HRV, stress, and sleep are genuinely stable for a week, the free version will output the same or similar protocols. If they are not, the app will change. If you want the engine to learn and stop jumping, that is what the paid version was designed for.

**Is the free version useless?**

Not at all. The free version is excellent for understanding how your daily biometrics affect a therapeutic soundscape, experimenting with different palettes and styles, and getting a fresh, data-driven session every night. It is just not designed to target chronic patterns or to stabilise your protocol over time — for that, you would need the paid version.
